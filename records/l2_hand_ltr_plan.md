# Offline test of the transfer term L_tr of the hand's role loss (pre-registration; analysis, no training run)

Written 2026-11-16 before running; approved by the user (handoff section 8, item 3). Platform: this PC's CPU (rollouts through the parallel launcher,
script/parallel_run.py; hand training in the parent process), under script/safe_run.sh. Results are compared only within this test.

## Question
In attempt 3 and in pilot O the hand's transfer residual drifts (median 0.44 at 30 -> 4.43 at 300 in attempt 3; arm D of pilot O did not stop it by a
decaying learning rate). Hypothesis (spec sec:l2a3): the role loss only measures the error inside rollouts in which the memory recalibrates at every rest,
so it does not pin how a calibration made at one posture transfers to another. Does adding the transfer term
  L_tr = E_{a,b} || G(q_b)^T (abar + z_a*) - u*_b ||^2,   z_a* = G(q_a) (G(q_a)^T G(q_a) + eps I)^{-1} (u*_a - G(q_a)^T abar)
to the role loss lower the transfer residual without raising the role loss by more than 10%?

## Design (the framework of eval/diagnostics/l2_hand_fresh.py)
- Brains and start hands (each brain with its own hand and the hand's optimizer state from the same checkpoint):
  B140 = data/runs/20261115134229/l2_attempt3/params_it0140.pkl; C200 = data/runs/20261116012508/l2_pilotO_C/params_it0200.pkl;
  N200 = data/runs/20261116012508/l2_pilotO_N/params_it0200.pkl.
- Data: 30 iterations; every iteration a fresh batch of 128 episodes from the training pool with restart share 0.5 (64 original, 32 phase-2, 32 phase-3
  starts), pool indices from default_rng(1000 + it), rollout keys split(PRNGKey(5000 + it)), sampled policy (mode 0), generated by the fixed brain and its
  start hand (as in l2_hand_fresh.py). The same batches for both arms.
- Arms (one copy of the start hand each, trained as in l2_train: 16 Adam steps per iteration on 32 episodes of the batch, lr 1e-3, gradient clip 1.0,
  minibatch indices from default_rng(7) per arm):
  - role: the role loss of training (variant 'all' of l2_hand_contact_core, equal to l2.replay_loss with the role weighting; checked on the first batch).
  - role+tr: role loss + 1.0 * L_tr on the same minibatch.
- L_tr on a minibatch: in each of the 32 episodes, 64 pairs (a, b) of control steps drawn uniformly (jax.random, key PRNGKey(9000 + step count)) and kept
  only if both steps are valid and have the same attachment state (xi), so that u* refers to the same load; u* = the recorded privileged target ustar
  (g(q) - d). eps = 1e-3 trace(G^T G) / 3 + 1e-9 (the damping of the rest-gated memory). L_tr = sum over kept pairs of the squared error / number of kept
  pairs (per episode, then the mean over episodes). The weight 1.0 is fixed here and not tuned.
- Measures (per brain and arm): median transfer residual of l2.hand_diagnostics on the 64 evaluation episodes at iterations 0, 5, 10, ..., 30; role loss =
  the role-loss value (variant 'all') on the training minibatches, mean over the last 80 steps (iterations 26-30), for both arms (for role+tr its role
  component alone); L_tr on the same steps.

## Decision rule (per brain)
L_tr passes on a brain if both hold:
  (a) the mean of the median transfer residual over the evaluations at 5, 10, ..., 30 of role+tr is below 1.768 N m and below that of role;
  (b) the role loss of role+tr (mean over the last 80 steps) is at most 1.10 times that of role.
L_tr is proposed for attempt 4 only if it passes on all three brains. Otherwise the outcome is descriptive and further measures are proposed, not added.

for each of the three checkpoints, one after another; outputs appended to records/l2_hand_ltr.json (key: run basename:iteration).
